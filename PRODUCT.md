## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS/JS, generated by `tools/build.js` from `data/*.js` (no framework, no runtime dependency). Output is the repo folder itself (`index.html`, `<slug>/index.html`, `clinic/<hub>/index.html`).

## Users

Patients (and parents of students, family of older adults) in Korea, mostly Seoul/Gyeonggi but also nationwide, who have repeated headaches, dizziness, palpitations/insomnia, "brain fog", poor concentration or memory worries. They often do not know the disease name, have had normal MRI/other tests, and arrive by search or YouTube/blog. They read on phones first.

## Product Purpose

Website of 강남뇌편한의원 (Gangnam BrainNeuro ease Korean Medicine), Seonneung Station, Seoul. Helps a visitor start from a symptom, understand likely differential causes and safety signals, see how the clinic examines and treats, trust the clinic, and book via Naver Booking, KakaoTalk or phone.

## Positioning

Symptom-first, differential-aware neuro/autonomic clinic: integrates Korean medicine with osteopathic training, EEG/autonomic/thermography/vascular tests and one-to-one follow-up; patients come from all over the country (500-patient residence distribution on hand).

## Product Principles

1. Start from the symptom, not the diagnosis.
2. Differentiate and be honest about limits: safety signals and standard care first.
3. Show the real clinic: real people, rooms, devices; never invented proof.
4. One clear action: book on Naver, with KakaoTalk and phone as easy alternates.
5. Phone-first reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable Korean at larger sizes for older family members; high contrast; focus states; works without JS for content.
